Privacy Policy

My Planner · Effective November 8, 2026

The short version
  • Most of your information stays on your iPhone.
  • We only send information off your phone to make a feature you turned on work.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can sign out, delete your account, or erase everything from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

My Planner is an iPhone app for tasks, your calendar and focus time, made by Jack Pedregon ("we"). Questions about this policy: jack@pedregon.com.

Who else handles your information

  • Supabase hosts our server and database. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • Anthropic runs Claude, the model behind the assistant, and processes your assistant requests. See the Anthropic privacy policy.
  • Google provides calendar colors when you sign in with Google. See the Google privacy policy.
  • Apple provides the Calendar, speech recognition, notifications and Screen Time features the app uses. See the Apple privacy policy.
  • GitHub hosts this page and the friend lock page. GitHub may log visits. See the GitHub privacy statement.

We share information with these services only to run the features you use. We never sell it.

Google user data

My Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google Calendar data only to show event colors and to change an event's color when you ask. We don't transfer it to anyone else, use it for ads, or let people read it.

App blocking and Screen Time

When app blocking is available, it uses Apple's Screen Time tools. Apple keeps the list of apps you choose on your iPhone and doesn't tell us which apps they are or how you use them.

How long we keep things

  • Friend lock records: deleted within 7 days.
  • Your account: until you delete it in the app (Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete My Account).
  • Everything on your iPhone: until you delete it, erase it in the app, or delete the app.

Your choices

In the app, go to Settings → Privacy & Security to sign out of everything, delete your account and the server's records about you, or erase everything the app saved on your iPhone. You can also turn off calendar, notification, microphone and other permissions any time in your iPhone's Settings, or email us to ask what we hold about you.

Security

Sign-ins and secret links are kept in the iPhone Keychain and aren't included in backups. Everything sent between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Our database blocks outside access, and the Claude API key is kept on the server, never in the app.

Children

My Planner isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For bigger changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
